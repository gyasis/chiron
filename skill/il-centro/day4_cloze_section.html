Il dialogo — 30 verbi da coniugare

This is the assignment itself, pages two and three of the worksheet. Every blank is a verb, and between them they cover the entire lesson: the regular -are/-ere/-ire, the reflexives, and the irregulars that refuse to follow. Click a blank to reveal it.
